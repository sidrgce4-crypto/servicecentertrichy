Why Regular Care Is Important for Your Kenstar Microwave

Taking proper care of your Kenstar microwave oven ensures long-lasting performance and keeps your kitchen safe in Trichy. Always wipe down food splatters from interior walls and the mica waveguide cover immediately, as burnt food particles create electrical arcing that damages the magnetron. Avoid operating the microwave completely empty, and only use certified microwave-safe glassware or ceramics. Keep enough space around the top and rear vents so internal cooling fans can release heat easily. If you notice persistent humming or sluggish heating, booking an inspection early prevents costly transformer or control board failure. Practicing simple daily kitchen maintenance protects your appliance investment for years.

What is the visiting charge for Kenstar microwave oven inspection in Trichy?

Why is my Kenstar microwave running but not heating food at all?

If the timer counts down and the light turns on but food remains cold, the failure is in the high-voltage heating circuit—commonly a burnt magnetron tube, a blown high-voltage fuse, a defective high-voltage diode, or capacitor failure.

What causes sparks or burning smells inside the Kenstar microwave cavity?

Sparks (arcing) inside the cooking cavity are usually caused by a burnt, greasy, or carbonized mica waveguide cover sheet, chipped cavity paint, or metal residue on the rack. Replacing the mica sheet and cleaning the cavity fixes the sparking safely.

Can you fix a Kenstar microwave oven touchpad that does not respond?

Yes. Touchpad unresponsiveness happens when the flexible membrane circuit ribbon wears out from humidity and grease, or when individual touch button tracks crack. We replace the membrane keypad or repair the control board.

Why has the glass turntable stopped rotating in my Kenstar microwave?

Turntable failure is caused by a burnt low-RPM synchronous turntable motor underneath the bottom panel, a stripped roller ring guide, or a broken plastic drive coupler.

What if the Kenstar microwave trips the home power breaker as soon as I press Start?

Tripping the circuit breaker immediately on start is almost always caused by a shorted door interlock microswitch, a shorted high-voltage capacitor, or a grounded transformer. Our technician tests each switch with an ohmmeter.

How much does magnetron replacement cost for a Kenstar microwave oven in Trichy?

Magnetron replacement typically ranges between ₹1,250 and ₹2,400 depending on the power rating (700W, 800W, 900W) and mounting bracket orientation. Final cost depends on the model, fault, and the part needed.

Is it safe to repair a microwave oven at home?

Microwave ovens store high electrical voltage in their capacitors even when unplugged. Our technicians safely discharge the capacitor using insulated grounding tools before inspecting components, making sure your home is completely safe.

Do you service convection baking mode problems in Kenstar microwaves?

Yes. If the convection fan does not blow hot air, the baking temperature fails to rise, or the heating rod stays cold, we check the upper heating element, convection blower motor, and thermal thermostat cut-offs.

How long does a microwave repair visit take?

Most repairs like mica sheet replacement, door switch change, turntable motor replacement, or high-voltage diode replacement are completed in 30 to 60 minutes on-site.
