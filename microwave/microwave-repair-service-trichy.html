Microwave Parts We Repair or Replace in Trichy

We inspect and service the following components where applicable. Component repair or replacement depends on the exact fault, condition, and model.

⚡ Magnetron Tube

The core electromagnetic generator tube that produces cooking waves. Tested for open filaments, burnt antenna caps, and internal shorts.

🔋 High-Voltage Capacitor

Stores high electrical charge to double voltage for the magnetron. Tested with capacitance meters and safely discharged before service.

🔌 High-Voltage Diode

Converts AC voltage into high DC voltage for heating. Replaced when shorted, open, or causing high-voltage fuse failure.

🛡️ Mica Waveguide Cover

Protects the magnetron antenna from food splatters and steam. Replaced when burned or carbonized to eliminate dangerous cavity sparking.

🔄 Turntable Synchronous Motor

Rotates the glass plate at steady RPM for even cooking. Replaced when gears strip, coils burn, or rotation stutters.

🚪 Door Safety Interlock Switches

Primary, secondary, and monitor micro-switches that stop operation when door opens. Replaced when contacts burn or stick.

🖥️ Electronic Control Board (PCB)

Coordinates cooking timers, power levels, and relays. Repaired at component level or replaced for relay and power supply faults.

🔘 Touch Keypad Membrane

Flexible conductive keypad on the front panel. Cleaned, serviced, or replaced when buttons refuse to register touches.

🌬️ Cooling & Convection Fans

Exhausts hot air from the magnetron and circulates heat in convection mode. Serviced or replaced for quiet, reliable airflow.

🍗 Grill & Convection Elements

Quartz and sheath heating elements for browning and baking. Tested for resistance continuity and replaced when open-circuited.

🌡️ Thermal Fuse & Thermostats

Safety temperature cut-offs mounted on magnetron and cavity. Replaced when tripped to restore normal power connectivity.

💡 Interior Light & Glass Plate

Cavity illumination lamps, glass turntables, and roller support rings replaced for clear visibility and smooth tray motion.

Common Signs Your Microwave Needs Repair

Watch out for these early warning signs to avoid dangerous electrical shorts or complete appliance breakdown.

⚠️ Cold Food After Cooking The light turns on and tray turns, but dishes remain stone cold after several minutes. Indicates high-voltage diode, capacitor, or magnetron failure.
⚠️ Visible Sparking or Flash Crackling blue or yellow sparks inside the right wall indicate carbonized food on the mica sheet. Stop using immediately to prevent magnetron damage.
⚠️ Jerky or Stationary Glass Plate If the tray wobbles, grinds, or stays stationary, food cooks unevenly with cold spots. Points to a worn coupler or failing turntable motor.
⚠️ Heavy Burning Electrical Smell A sharp burning odor indicates overheated transformer insulation, burnt wiring, or plastic coupler friction. Unplug the microwave safely.
⚠️ Unresponsive Start / Number Keys Touch buttons fail to register or beep without starting. Caused by kitchen steam degrading the conductive membrane tracks on the front control panel.
⚠️ Door Must Be Pushed Hard to Start If the oven only starts when the door is slammed or held, the internal door micro-switches or latch hooks are worn and need realignment.

Frequently Asked Questions About Microwave Oven Repair in Trichy

Helpful answers regarding doorstep checking, repairs, spare parts, and visiting charges in Trichy.

Do you repair microwave ovens at home in Trichy?

Yes, we provide doorstep repair services for solo, grill, and convection microwave ovens throughout Trichy, including Srirangam, Thillai Nagar, KK Nagar, Cantonment, and Kattur.

What is the visiting charge for microwave oven inspection in Trichy?

Our standard home visit inspection fee is ₹249 to ₹349 across Trichy. The technician tests the magnetron, high-voltage diode, transformer, door safety switches, and keypad membrane. The fee is adjusted against the repair charge if you proceed.

Why is my microwave running but not heating food at all?

If the timer counts down and the light turns on but food remains cold, the failure is in the high-voltage heating circuit—commonly a burnt magnetron tube, a blown high-voltage fuse, a defective high-voltage diode, or capacitor failure.

What causes sparks or burning smells inside the microwave cavity?

Sparks (arcing) inside the cooking cavity are usually caused by a burnt, greasy, or carbonized mica waveguide cover sheet, chipped cavity paint, or metal residue on the rack. Replacing the mica sheet and cleaning the cavity fixes the sparking safely.

Can you fix a microwave oven touchpad that does not respond?

Yes. Touchpad unresponsiveness happens when the flexible membrane circuit ribbon wears out from humidity and grease, or when individual touch button tracks crack. We replace the membrane keypad or repair the control board.

Why has the glass turntable stopped rotating in my microwave?

Turntable failure is caused by a burnt low-RPM synchronous turntable motor underneath the bottom panel, a stripped roller ring guide, or a broken plastic drive coupler.

What if the microwave trips the home power breaker as soon as I press Start?

Tripping the circuit breaker immediately on start is almost always caused by a shorted door interlock microswitch, a shorted high-voltage capacitor, or a grounded transformer. Our technician tests each switch with an ohmmeter.

How much does magnetron replacement cost for a microwave oven in Trichy?

Magnetron replacement typically ranges between ₹1,250 and ₹2,400 depending on the power rating (700W, 800W, 900W) and mounting bracket orientation. Final cost depends on the model, fault, and the part needed.

Is it safe to repair a microwave oven at home?

Microwave ovens store high electrical voltage in their capacitors even when unplugged. Our technicians safely discharge the capacitor using insulated grounding tools before inspecting components, making sure your home is completely safe.

Do you service convection baking mode problems in microwaves?

Yes. If the convection fan does not blow hot air, the baking temperature fails to rise, or the heating rod stays cold, we check the upper heating element, convection blower motor, and thermal thermostat cut-offs.

How long does a microwave repair visit take?

Most repairs like mica sheet replacement, door switch change, turntable motor replacement, or high-voltage diode replacement are completed in 30 to 60 minutes on-site.
