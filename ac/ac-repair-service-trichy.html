Split air conditioners are the most popular cooling systems in Trichy homes because of their quiet indoor operation, elegant look, and energy efficiency.

Inverter AC Repair Service in Trichy

Need inverter AC repair in Trichy? Modern inverter air conditioners use variable-speed BLDC compressors and modern electronic controller boards (PCBs) to regulate room temperature smoothly while reducing monthly power consumption. However, because inverter systems rely heavily on sensitive microcontrollers, voltage fluctuations and ambient humidity can cause circuit faults.

When an inverter AC malfunctions, it typically flashes an error code on the indoor LED display or stops cooling without warning. Common faults include communication loss between indoor and outdoor PCB modules, shorted IPM (Intelligent Power Module) drive transistors, defective ambient/tube thermistors, or seized electronic expansion valves (EEVs). Unlike conventional ACs, inverter systems need precise multimeter checks, component-level testing, and exact refrigerant gram weighing during gas recharge. Our technicians are trained to check inverter error codes accurately and perform board repairs directly at your premises whenever possible.

Common Inverter AC Solutions We Provide:

Outdoor drive PCB repair and replacement, IPM power module troubleshooting, indoor-outdoor communication wire repair, digital sensor thermistor replacement, electronic expansion valve testing, and R32/R410A precision vacuum charging.

Why Regular AC Service Is Important

Because Trichy is situated close to the coast, airborne dust and salty moisture continuously pass through your air conditioner's indoor cooling coils and outdoor condenser fins. Over months of regular use, dirt builds up on air filters and restricts essential airflow. When airflow is choked, the cooling coil freezes into solid ice, water drips onto floors, and the compressor is forced to run continuously under high discharge pressure, leading to steep electricity bills and premature motor burnout. Routine servicing and seasonal jet cleaning remove dirt, clear clogged drain channels, protect copper coils from salt corrosion, and allow technicians to spot weak capacitors or slow gas leaks before a sudden cooling breakdown occurs.

Why is my AC blowing warm air instead of cooling?

Warm air from the indoor unit usually points to a burnt compressor capacitor, a refrigerant gas leak at copper pipe joints, a choked cooling coil, or an outdoor unit PCB fault. Our technician checks gas pressure and electrical parts on-site to pinpoint the exact issue.

Can you fix indoor unit water leakage from split AC?

Yes. Water dripping down bedroom walls or from the front blower is commonly caused by algae or dust choking the condensate drain pipe, a tilted indoor unit bracket, or ice melting due to low refrigerant. We clear the drain line and flush the tray during the visit.

Do you repair inverter AC models in Trichy?

Yes. We service both conventional fixed-speed and modern inverter AC models. Our technicians test inverter PCB circuit boards, BLDC fan motors, sensor thermistors, and electronic expansion valves.

How much does AC gas charging cost in Trichy?

Refrigerant gas filling typically ranges from ₹1,450 to ₹2,850 depending on the refrigerant type (R32, R410A, or R22), tonnage (1 ton, 1.5 ton, 2 ton), and whether nitrogen leak testing and copper brazing are needed. Final price depends on the fault and part needed.

Do you offer foam or pressure jet wash cleaning for AC?

Yes. For ACs clogged with dust and lint, we provide thorough pressure jet servicing with indoor service jackets to protect your walls. Deep jet cleaning restores proper airflow, improves cooling, and reduces electricity consumption.

What if my AC trips the household MCB breaker repeatedly?

Breaker tripping usually indicates a grounded compressor winding, a short-circuited dual capacitor, or high inrush current due to a jammed fan motor. Our technician tests insulation resistance with a meter before attempting any startup.

How long does an AC repair visit take?
