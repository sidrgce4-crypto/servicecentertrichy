LED televisions are the most common TVs in Trichy households. While they offer slim designs and low power consumption, the tiny LED backlight strips inside the screen run hot over hours of daily use and can burn out.

Common LED TV Problems We Repair

  • Sound Playing but No Picture: The most common LED TV fault. Audio works normally, but screen is pitch black or displays a faint shadow when illuminated with a torch.
  • TV Not Turning On / Dead TV: Power indicator light is completely off, often caused by a power surge damaging the power supply board.
  • Red Standby Light Blinking: TV stays in protection mode and fails to boot into normal operation.
  • Dark Spots or Dim Display: One or more LED beads on the backlight strip have burnt out, creating dark patches on the screen.
  • Screen Flickering: Rapid brightness fluctuations caused by failing LED driver circuits or loose ribbon cables.
  • Lines on Screen: Thin horizontal or vertical colored lines caused by loose T-Con ribbon cables or display panel tab issues.
  • HDMI Ports Not Working: Setup box or gaming console signal not showing on screen due to loose or shorted HDMI ports.

Older LCD televisions use cold cathode fluorescent lamps (CCFL) rather than LED beads. Because these TVs use high-voltage inverter boards to strike the lamps, component heating over 8 to 12 years can cause transformer failure, blown electrolytic capacitors, or lamp degradation.

Common LCD TV Problems We Check

  • TV Shuts Down After 2 Seconds: Inverter board senses a failing CCFL tube and immediately activates protection shutdown.
  • Red or Pinkish Tint at Startup: Indicates CCFL backlight tubes are near end-of-life.
  • Blown Capacitors: Bulging capacitors on the power supply board causing clicking sounds and delayed startup.
  • No Video Input Display: Aging AV/HDMI video processor chip faults on the main board.

Our technicians test LCD inverter transformers, replace capacitors, and service power supply boards across all Trichy locations.

Why does my TV have sound but no picture (dark/black screen)?

Sound without picture is a very common issue on LED TVs caused by failure of the LED backlight strips inside the display panel. When one or more LEDs burn out, the backlight power driver shuts down. We test and replace the LED backlight strips to restore picture clarity.

Can you repair a TV with vertical or horizontal lines on the screen?

Horizontal or vertical lines can be caused by loose T-Con board ribbon cables, a faulty timing controller (T-Con) board, or damaged display panel COF (chip-on-film) IC bonds. While T-Con board faults can be repaired, physical panel glass damage cannot always be fixed.

What if my TV does not turn on at all and the standby light is off?

If there is no standby red light and no response, the power supply board (SMPS) has likely suffered a power surge, blown fuse, or shorted MOSFET capacitor. We carry out component-level power board repair on-site.

Do you repair Smart TV motherboard and Wi-Fi connection issues?

Yes. If your Smart TV gets stuck on the brand logo screen, restarts repeatedly, or fails to connect to home Wi-Fi, our technician inspects the mainboard, firmware chip, and internal Wi-Fi receiver module.

Can broken or cracked TV screens be repaired?

If the outer or inner LCD glass panel is physically cracked or broken due to an impact, the glass panel itself cannot be patched. Panel replacement costs often approach the price of a new television. We explain this honestly during inspection.

How much does TV LED backlight replacement cost in Trichy?

Complete LED backlight strip replacement generally ranges between ₹1,450 and ₹3,500 depending on screen size (32-inch, 43-inch, 55-inch) and panel design. Final cost depends on screen size, fault, and the specific part needed.

What if the TV speakers crackle or there is no audio output?

No sound or distorted crackling is typically caused by torn speaker paper cones, burnt audio amplifier ICs on the main motherboard, or incorrect audio output settings. We carry compatible replacement speaker units.

Do you provide TV wall-mount unmounting and installation in Trichy?

Yes. We provide standard wall-mounting, swivel-bracket installation, and unmounting services for all LED and Smart TV sizes with proper drill anchoring and cable concealment.

How long does an in-home TV repair take?

Power board repairs, speaker replacements, cable reseating, and software resets take 45 to 75 minutes at home. Backlight replacement needs careful panel disassembly on a clean flat surface and takes around 90 to 120 minutes.
