Hisense TV Standby & Indicator Light Warnings

Hisense televisions indicate circuit status through the front standby LED sequences:

  • Alternating Green/Red Blink: Power supply board secondary DC rail overload or protection shutdown.
  • Red Light Flashes 3 Times: Inverter board or LED backlight open circuit failure.
  • Stuck on Hisense Logo: Main processor firmware boot failure or eMMC memory corruption.
  • Relay Click Cycle: SMPS attempting startup and failing back into safety protection mode.

Note: Error messages and indicator codes can vary by model. Check the exact model number before assuming the fault.

Why does my Hisense TV have sound but no picture (dark/black screen)?

Sound without picture is a very common issue on LED TVs caused by failure of the LED backlight strips inside the display panel. When one or more LEDs burn out, the backlight power driver shuts down. We test and replace the LED backlight strips to restore picture clarity.

Can you repair a Hisense TV with vertical or horizontal lines on the screen?

Horizontal or vertical lines can be caused by loose T-Con board ribbon cables, a faulty timing controller (T-Con) board, or damaged display panel COF (chip-on-film) IC bonds. While T-Con board faults can be repaired, physical panel glass damage cannot always be fixed.

What if my Hisense TV does not turn on at all and the standby light is off?

If there is no standby red light and no response, the power supply board (SMPS) has likely suffered a power surge, blown fuse, or shorted MOSFET capacitor. We carry out component-level power board repair on-site.

Do you repair Hisense Smart TV motherboard and Wi-Fi connection issues?

Yes. If your Smart TV gets stuck on the brand logo screen, restarts repeatedly, or fails to connect to home Wi-Fi, our technician inspects the mainboard, firmware chip, and internal Wi-Fi receiver module.

Can broken or cracked Hisense TV screens be repaired?

If the outer or inner LCD glass panel is physically cracked or broken due to an impact, the glass panel itself cannot be patched. Panel replacement costs often approach the price of a new television. We explain this honestly during inspection.

How much does Hisense TV LED backlight replacement cost in Trichy?

Complete LED backlight strip replacement generally ranges between ₹1,450 and ₹3,500 depending on screen size (32-inch, 43-inch, 55-inch) and panel design. Final cost depends on screen size, fault, and the specific part required.

What if the TV speakers crackle or there is no audio output?

No sound or distorted crackling is typically caused by torn speaker paper cones, burnt audio amplifier ICs on the main motherboard, or incorrect audio output settings. We carry compatible replacement speaker units.

Do you provide TV wall-mount unmounting and installation in Trichy?

Yes. We provide standard wall-mounting, swivel-bracket installation, and unmounting services for all LED and Smart TV sizes with proper drill anchoring and cable concealment.

How long does an in-home Hisense TV repair take?

Power board repairs, speaker replacements, cable reseating, and software resets take 45 to 75 minutes at home. Backlight replacement requires careful panel disassembly on a clean flat surface and takes around 90 to 120 minutes.
